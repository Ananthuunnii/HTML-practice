IT, finance firms among targets of rogue Anthropic, OpenAI agents

AI agents are autonomous systems capable of independently planning and executing tasks, accessing external websites and software tools, and taking actions with limited human intervention. Unlike conventional chatbots, which primarily respond to queries, such systems can perform a series of actions without requiring human instructions at every stage. The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (Cert-In), which disclosed last week that AI agents had accessed confidential business records of organisations while operating beyond their assigned tasks, has conducted a preliminary assessment of the incidents. The damage caused was relatively limited, because the AI companies identified the unauthorised activity during internal reviews and alerted the Indian government in time, the official said.

Anahat gets the GOAT: Beats 8-time world champ Nour El Sherbini in California

In Nour El Sherbini, Anahat Singh on Thursday faced a woman regarded by many as squash’s greatest of all time. Yet, going into the match, India head coach Harinder Pal Sandhu believed the teenager “definitely does have a shot” against the legend. An obvious reason for confidence was the junior world champion’s recent performances and a propensity to punch above her ranking. Another one was El Sherbini’s patchy form: she had lost four of her last seven matches on tour and didn’t look quite like herself this season. As it turned out, Anahat proved Harinder right emphatically. Undaunted by her opponent’s reputation and her own reverence, the Indian unleashed the killer instinct that has made her one of the sport’s most exciting talents.
